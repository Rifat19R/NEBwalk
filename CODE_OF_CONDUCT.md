# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, welcome good-faith questions, give
specific and constructive technical feedback, and distinguish evidence from
opinion. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption
are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and as promptly as practical. Responses may include a warning,
content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and public representation of the project.
